
# 🎓 Student Performance Analyzer

## 📌 Overview

The **Student Performance Analyzer** is a Generative AI-based academic assistance system designed to help students understand their academic performance and enable faculty to analyze student progress efficiently.

The system provides separate interfaces for students and faculty, combining academic data analysis with an AI-powered chatbot to deliver personalized insights, study recommendations, and academic support.

Students can analyze their marks, attendance, and assignment scores, while faculty can upload academic records, identify students who need attention, analyze subject-wise performance, and generate class-level insights.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Student academic data is often scattered across marksheets, attendance registers, and learning platforms. As a result, students who are struggling may not be identified early enough to receive meaningful support.

Faculty members also lack a quick and explainable way to understand why a student is underperforming and what actions could help. Existing tools mainly display raw numbers without providing personalized guidance or actionable recommendations.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The Student Performance Analyzer combines academic data analysis and Generative AI to transform raw academic information into meaningful insights.

- Students can enter their academic details and receive personalized performance analysis.
- Faculty can upload Excel sheets containing student records for automated analysis.
- An AI-powered chatbot provides conversational academic assistance.
- The system identifies weak subjects and students requiring attention.
- Course materials and academic documents can be used to provide context-based answers.

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student Module
- Enter marks, attendance, assignment scores, and other academic details.
- View academic performance summaries.
- Identify weak subjects and areas requiring improvement.
- Receive personalized study suggestions.
- Generate improvement plans.
- Interact with an AI chatbot for academic guidance.

### 👩‍🏫 Faculty Module
- Upload Excel sheets containing student academic records.
- Automatically process academic data and calculate statistics.
- Analyze subject-wise student performance.
- Identify students requiring additional attention.
- Generate overall class performance reviews.
- Ask AI-powered questions about individual students or the entire class.
- Receive data-based observations and academic support recommendations.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Assistance
- Conversational interaction for students and faculty.
- Personalized academic insights.
- Study recommendations and improvement strategies.
- Data-based performance summaries.
- Context-aware responses using uploaded course materials and academic documents.

## 🔄 System Workflow

1. **Data Input** – Students enter their academic details, while faculty upload Excel files containing student records.
2. **Data Processing** – The backend validates academic records and calculates performance statistics.
3. **Performance Analysis** – The system analyzes marks, attendance, assignments, and subject-wise results.
4. **AI Insights** – Generative AI produces summaries, observations, and recommendations based on the academic data.
5. **Chatbot Interaction** – Students and faculty interact with the AI chatbot to obtain personalized insights.
6. **Academic Support** – Students receive improvement suggestions, while faculty obtain insights to support students more effectively.
